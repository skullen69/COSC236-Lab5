3 Possible Outcomes That May Occur During Borrowing or Returning:
	1. If such a system was to exist, membership's could expire and have deadlines for expiration. Expired memberships cause users to be unable to borrow anymore books until their membership is renewed. To prevent this, A member's membership status could be checked each time they borrow, if it close to expiration, that member must renew on the spot. This would likely impact BorrowingService mainly, as it would be used to check if a membership is expired, but it likely wouldn't need a new class or abstraction for specifically checking memberships.
	2. A member could have multiple instances where they fail to renew their membership or return books that are overdue, displaying constant failure to obide by the rules of the library's system. This can be avoided by having some function to blacklist the member from borrowing or returning books, however this would likely be easier to implement by having some sort of boolean property defined for each member, which BorrowingService could use to determine if this member can borrow books or not.
	3. A Library may only have a certain amount of books in a catalog, and there may be a instance where there is more members currently than there is books. Which could also mean that every book could be unavailable, leaving there nothing to borrow for other members. This could be avoided by having a member limit that will never be more than the amount of books that could be available. This would require very little modification and would likely only require a few changes to the Library class.
